# Brand voice: Sorted by Us

Source: copy the owner approved or rejected on the live site, the positioning work against the How&How
branding handbook, and the copy research in `docs/copy-research.md`. Last updated: 19 November 2026. Used by the `site-copy` skill: every line of site copy
should trace back to this file.

## Audience
Two paying customers with one need, someone they trust on the ground in Nigeria:
- **In Nigeria** (Lagos, Abuja): professionals and families who know how things work but don't have the hours.
  They're buying time back.
- **Abroad** (London, Houston, Toronto…): Nigerians organising things for family at home who lack reach and
  trusted people on the ground. They're buying eyes, hands and confidence at home.
Both are aspirational, not old money: they want ease and to feel looked after, and they trust openness about
price and process. Premium comes from the look and the care, not from gatekeeping.

## Voice
Warm, direct, reassuring, quietly confident, a little playful. A friend who's good at this, talking.

## The writing principle
**The details that matter to you are part of the request.** Not a tagline: the decision every line is written
from. Show that we listen for the person's taste, the occasion's mood, the exact item or the constraint they're
working around, and that it shapes what we do. ("Tell us what you need and what matters about it.")

**The role: a friend who's good at this.** In practice that friend:
- listens for the important detail,
- asks a sensible follow-up,
- explains a recommendation (why this option fits your brief),
- says what happens next,
- is honest about what's possible.
A paragraph should show at least one of these when it's relevant. Warmth comes from attention, not slang, jokes
or over-familiarity.

**Picture the outcome.** Say what the task makes possible: a birthday delivery shows someone you remembered;
a ready home means arriving comfortably; a dinner arranged means the host gets to be with the guests. Give each
service its own purpose rather than the same "handled / arranged / sorted".

**Dignity.** Clients in Lagos know their city; clients abroad are busy with lives elsewhere, not absent or
guilty. Copy extends what they can do; it never implies incompetence or guilt.

**Humour fits the moment.** A light observation can work around dinners, gifts and occasions. Payment,
prescriptions, delays, damage and anything going wrong get plain, direct language.

## The idea, and what we write against
- **The idea, said everywhere: "sorted".** It's in the name, "Consider it sorted.", "See what we sort", "What can
  we sort?". Don't introduce a second organising idea.
- **What you gain, not what you avoid.** Lead with the life it gives back ("More life, less admin", "Your time,
  back"); the hassle is the setup, never the headline.
- **The foil.** Concierge-world gatekeeping (VIP, bespoke, exclusive, "lifestyle management", "solutions") on one
  side; on the other, the chasing, queues, traffic and follow-ups that eat a Lagos week, and the worry of
  organising things at home from abroad. We're the opposite of both: open, warm, and on it.
- **Two registers.** Personality in headings and a few brand lines. Prices, process, payment, policy, errors and
  FAQs are plain, exact and calm.

## Messaging house
Draft for the owner to confirm. New pages draw from this rather than reinventing the pitch.
- **Tagline (the most repeated line):** Consider it sorted.
- **One-line descriptor:** Your personal concierge in Nigeria, for people living here and abroad.
- **Short pitch:** Tell us what you need and what matters about it. We find the right options, recommend the one
  we'd choose and see it through, with updates on WhatsApp until it's done. You see one quote, our fee included,
  before anything goes ahead.
- **Points of difference, each with its proof:**
  1. *We recommend, not just find.* Proof: the options-plus-recommendation quote. (Needs a real example.)
  2. *You see it done.* Proof: photos at every step and receipts. (Needs a real example or two.)
  3. *Here or away.* Proof: WhatsApp updates whatever your time zone; most Lagos requests sorted the same day.
  PENDING: "One person, start to finish" would be the strongest point if it's operationally true.
- **Missing proof the site needs:** reviews or client quotes, how many requests sorted, the founder's name on
  About, and real request stories (with permission).

## Customer vocabulary
Their words for their moments. Use them where they're true; never fake Pidgin or put on an accent.
aso-ebi · owambe · Detty December · back home · Mum / Mummy and Daddy · the Island / the Mainland · Lagos traffic ·
"before I land" · "while I'm away" · "for the family house" · suya, jollof, small chops · Tarkwa Bay, Lekki, Surulere

## Anti-examples (rejected, and why)
- "We love finding solutions." / "Discuss a corporate solution": corporate, says nothing.
- "The table that's fully booked. The part no one stocks. The queue at the office. Tell us once.": a run of
  clipped fragments reads robotic. Say it as one person would.
- "The one who knows who to call" as a section heading: floaty, doesn't say what the section is. (It works
  inside the founder's story on About.)
- "Here in Nigeria or abroad, one message and it's handled." as a hero subline: the owner didn't like a
  tagline under the hero heading.
- Exclusive, VIP, members-only, luxury tones: not who we are.
- App-interface voice (notifications, quote bubbles, "Your request has been submitted!").

## Hard constraints
- British spelling (colour, organise, realise).
- Curly apostrophes and quotes (’ “ ”). No exclamation marks, no emoji. Em dashes rarely; a comma or full stop
  usually reads better.
- Sentence case everywhere, buttons included. Contractions always.
- Headings: say what the section is, in 7 words or fewer. Short brand lines: 5 words or fewer, and rare. Only
  "More life, less admin" is set on staggered levels; keep it that way unless the owner chooses another.
- Real places, spelled right: Lagos (Island, Mainland, Lekki, Surulere), Abuja, Tarkwa Bay, Jos.
- Always "Sorted by Us" in full, never "Sorted" on its own: The Sorted Concierge is a Lagos competitor with a
  near-identical name.
- Plain adult reading level. Fees and payment can be visible, never the first thing someone reads.

## Claims, held verbatim
- "Most Lagos requests are sorted the same day." Never "always", "guaranteed" or "within hours".
- "One quote, our fee included, before anything goes ahead."
- Payment: "by bank transfer, once you've approved the quote. We'll never ask for card details online."
- Proof: "photos so you know it's done" / "photos and receipts".
- Reply time (forms): "usually within the hour during the day". Never "instantly" or "24/7".
- Don't embellish a claim in passing: "Most Lagos gifts delivered the same day, timed to the minute" turned the
  same-day claim into a precision promise. Add nothing to a claim that the claim doesn't say.
- PENDING owner confirmation, don't use on new copy: "One person handles your request from start to finish, and
  knows you next time." (It's on About already.)
- Placeholders in [square brackets] on How it works (time windows, returns and cancellation policy, paying from
  abroad) are for the owner to fill. Never invent them.

## Banned for this brand
- solution (corporate; say what we actually do)
- solutions
- coordination fee (say "our fee")
- hassle-free (cliché)
- exclusive (gatekeeping, not our audience)
- VIP
- elite
- luxury
- urgent (in the interface; "same day" reads calmer)
- simply
- to the minute (an unbacked precision promise)

## Writing with the design
Copy is written alongside the request cards, photos, quote format and client messages, not added at the end.
Pair each photo with an intention that fits it (flowers with a recipient-led brief, the clothes rail with an item
or preference, bunting with a gathering). An atmospheric photo needn't illustrate every word, but the
relationship should make sense.

## Maintenance
Review per release. When the owner approves a line, add it above; when they reject one, move it to
anti-examples with the reason.
