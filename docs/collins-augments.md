# Collins "augments": notes, and what we can use

Collins (wearecollins.com) ends each case study with a list of **augments**. In their words, these are
"add-on components or properties that enhance a design system's performance". Each one is a named
capability the identity system has, beyond the logo and colours.

**What was read (Nov 2026)**
- All 54 case studies listed on wearecollins.com/case-studies. Microsoft Copilot and Crocs are marked
  "coming soon".
- 48 of them have an augments section. Red Wing, Helios House, Target: A New Day, Chubb, Aldo and Equinox
  don't.
- The descriptions are short. Most don't say how an augment works, so the "how it works" notes below come
  from the case-study text and are thin in places.

## The augments, grouped

### 1. Making outside content look like ours
| Augment | Used by | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 3P IP Adapter: Frame | SF Symphony, sweetgreen, Twitch, YouTube Gaming | A brand shape frames third-party content. SF Symphony uses the moving wordmark, sweetgreen the shapes of its serving bowls, YouTube Gaming a game-HUD frame. |
| 3P IP Adapter: Lens | Spotify, Ogilvy | A treatment applied to any image. Spotify's duotone screen-print look makes any photo read as Spotify. |
| 3P IP Adapter: Distinctive Crop | OpenWeb | A signature crop applied to outside images. |
| 3P IP Adapter (no suffix) | ESPN, Freeform, Avon | The same idea, with no mechanism described. |
| Sandbox | Girl Scouts, Institute of Design, Archer Farms | A fixed container; anything can go inside it. |
| Community Canvas | Crane, Twitch | Customer and creator content brought into the system. |

### 6. Lasting and growing
| Augment | Used by | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Endurance Function | Cadia, Ogilvy, Titan, US Chamber, Babyganics, Avon, Andmore, Legence, Match, OYO, Anson Calder, Institute of Design, Guild | Timely and timeless; avoids trends. |

## What Sorted by Us has, and what to add

### Already in our system (named now, in `docs/identity-system.md`)
| Their augment | Ours |
|---|---|
| 3P IP Adapter: Frame | **The cut.** Every outside photo sits in a cut frame (Rule 1). |
| Sticky Dissonance, Vibrating Color | **Amber on umber.** One point of tension per page, on the hero (Rule 2). |
| High Expressive Ratio, Flex Deck | The cut, four earth colours and butter make the whole site. |
| Colorgraph | Partly: butter only on things you can press, earth colours in a fixed order. Worth writing out in full (see 4 below). |
| Verbal System | `docs/brand-voice.md` and the copy linter. |
| Endurance Function | Earth palette, serif headings, no trend effects. Keep it that way. |

### Worth adding, best first
1. **Memory Marker: the moment something turns butter means "sorted".**
   - We already do this on the map: a route lights butter, and the request tag fills butter when it's
     done.
   - Make that the one cue people remember, and use it everywhere a job finishes:
     - the "Sorted" pill on the WhatsApp completion message
     - the receipt
     - the confirmation screen after "Make a request"
   - It's cheap, because the parts exist already.
2. **Community Canvas, using our version of the Lens:**
   - The photos and receipts we send at the end of every job are proof, and they make good content.
   - Run them through the cut frame (Rule 1), with consent, as a "Recently sorted" strip on the homepage
     and on Instagram.
   - That turns customer photos into brand material without them looking like snapshots.
3. **CX Bridge: the identity carried into the service itself.**
   - Most of the experience happens on WhatsApp and in quotes, not on the site.
   - Design the pieces people actually receive in our look:
     - the WhatsApp profile and greeting
     - the quote (one total, fee included)
     - the photo update
     - the receipt
   - Use the same voice and the same "sorted" end state.
4. **Colorgraph: one page of colour rules.**
   - Which colour goes where: grounds, bands, blocks, actions, tension.
   - Whether each of the six service categories gets a fixed colour on the map, service pages and cards.
     Today they don't, and four earth colours don't stretch to six, so that needs a decision.
5. **Motion Signature: write ours down.**
   - The parts are there:
     - the hero block sliding in
     - routes drawing in butter on the map
     - the "rise" in the motion tokens
   - Name them as one signature, "a route draws in, then it lights", so new motion matches. The motion
     studio already generates from `motion/brand.json`.
6. **Culture Sync Logic: home and diaspora.**
   - We serve Lagos and Abuja, and people in London and Houston.
   - Set rules for what changes with the audience and what never does:
     - What changes: place names, currency and stories (the map already mixes local and diaspora requests).
     - What never changes: the cut, the colours and the "sorted" cue.
7. **Custom Icon System from the logo's line.**
   - The map lines are drawn like the logo: round caps, rounded turns.
   - Draw the service and CTA icons with that same stroke, much as Cadia built its icons from a loop
     motif. `docs/cta-icons.md` is the place to start.
8. **Hidden Story, for later.**
   - If the logo's "S" is read as a route, then the logo, the map and the icons all tell one story: we
     find the way.
   - A short animation of the mark drawing itself as a route would tie them together.
9. **Validated Versatility, before launch.**
   - Test the kit off the website:
     - WhatsApp avatar and status
     - Instagram post and story
     - quote PDF
     - gift tag and sticker for wrapped orders
     - email footer

### Skip for now
- **Shelf Pop, Choice Architecture, Decision Arc:** these are for packaging on a shelf, and we don't have any.
- **Heritage Capsule, Archive Revival:** we have no archive to revive.
- **Vertical and Horizontal Compatibility:** not until there's a sub-brand or a partner.
- **Custom or kinetic typeface:** expensive, and the current pair of fonts is doing the job.
